How we rank

When an offer gives a price and a size, we work out the price per gram (per 100 mg THC for edibles, tinctures and RSO), after any multi-buy terms, and list the lowest first. Offers we cannot turn into a unit price come after those, ordered by percent off as advertised. Nothing here is our opinion of a product.
